ZENO DESK · GESTURE DATA

Real recordings from the desk phone's front camera. Apple Vision finds 21 points on the hand in every frame (10 a second). Only these numbers are saved, never the image. A model learns gestures from how the points move.

What the camera saw, redrawn from the saved points
The skeleton is drawn at the hand's real position in the frame. Left and right are as you see them, facing the phone.
This frame = one row of the CSV
Joint positions are stored relative to the wrist and scaled by the hand's size, so the same pose gives the same numbers near or far from the camera. hand_x / hand_y say where the hand is, which is what a swipe changes.
Hand position across the recording hand_x (0 = your left, 1 = your right)   the 1-second window the model reads for one decision
A swipe is a ramp in hand_x. Palm and pinch hold still. "No gesture" wanders. Gaps are frames where no hand was seen.
